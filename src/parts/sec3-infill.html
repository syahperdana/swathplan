Coverage and gaps (infill)

Load what has been surveyed so far. The planner marks the covered parts of the area, finds the gaps (holidays) and plans infill lines across them.

GeoTIFFs in WGS 84 or WGS 84 / UTM are placed from their own EPSG code; other files use the coordinate system of section {§crs}. Use LZW, Deflate or no compression (not JPEG). Large files are read at reduced resolution.

Track lines (estimate before processing)

No tracks loaded.

From depth: the swath angles of section {§swath} and the depth along each track (loaded bathymetry, or the design depth). Port is left of the direction of travel.

Each track sees from the nadir gap under the towfish (about its height on each side) out to the range. At 200 %, every point must be seen by two tracks; points seen once show in yellow. Infill lines are spaced for the chosen coverage.
