No coastline loaded. Close an area first.

A loaded coastline is kept with the project, in the autosave and in saved .SPL files, so download the OSM coastline once while online and it keeps working offline. The OSM coastline is detailed and worldwide but user-contributed: check it against official charts. The built-in coastline is Natural Earth 1:10m, accurate to a few hundred metres (1:50m, about 1 km, when the page is used on its own without its data folder): fine for offshore and coastal planning, with a clearance to match, but not for harbours. For detailed offline work, load a GeoJSON export of GSHHG (full) or OSM land polygons. Turn paths are not checked against land.
