Drone payload & flight

Sensor size, focal length and image width follow the makers' published specifications. The shortest photo interval is the maker's figure where one is published, otherwise 1 s: check your camera. The image's long side is across the flight direction.

Presets follow the makers' published specifications (single-return point rate; repetitive or linear scanning across the flight direction). For a 360° scanner only the points within the downward field of view count, so the preset rate is that share. The RIEGL range is given at 20 % reflectivity. The L3's point rate follows its pulse rate, 100 kHz to 2 MHz: enter yours. The density is an average for flat ground; vegetation, water and dark surfaces return fewer points.
