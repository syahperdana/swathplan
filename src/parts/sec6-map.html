6. Map background & search

Google and Bing have no free keyless tile URL. Paste the tile URL from your own API account into Custom. Placeholders: {z} {x} {y}, {q} = Bing quadkey, {s} = subdomain a–c.

A PMTiles file is read in small pieces, so even a large file stays light on a phone. Convert MBTiles with the pmtiles tool, or download a file from a provider that allows offline use.

Map tiles you view are kept on this device for offline use. Before going offline, pan and zoom over your survey area at the scales you need.

Saves your Custom tile URL for the survey area. The built-in map servers do not allow bulk downloads.
