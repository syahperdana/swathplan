Compare scenarios (tender)

Save the current settings as a scenario, change what you want to compare (sensor, overlap, swath angle, speed, turn radius, day rate, working hours, vessels), and compare time, line length and cost on the same area or route.

Each scenario is planned with the same geometry and project settings as the planner. Days include the weather allowance, mob/demob and calibration from Project & operations. With several vessels the days are shared between them and each vessel is charged its own days.
