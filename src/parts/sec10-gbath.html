Global bathymetry (online, free)
Close an area first (draw, preset or import), then fetch.

The data are only as good as the source: GMRT and NCEI hold real multibeam surveys in places but are 100–450 m grids elsewhere, so use them for planning, not as a substitute for your own soundings. Land and drying cells are ignored. Vertical datum is roughly mean sea level, not chart datum: use the vertical offset in section {§xyz} or choose a conservative depth. If your browser blocks a service (offline or CORS), use the link shown to download the grid and load it here. Sources: GMRT (Lamont-Doherty), NOAA NCEI, EMODnet. Long routes and large areas are fetched in pieces around the survey area.
