Uncertainty budget (a priori TPU)

Enter the 1σ uncertainty of each part of the system. The planner combines them for every beam angle at the design depth and compares the 95 % result with the IHO S-44 order in section {§iho}.

A simplified a priori model after Hare (1995): range, angle (roll plus pointing), heave, water level, draft and sound speed for depth; positioning, heading, angle, pitch and sound speed for position. 95 % = 1.96σ vertical, 2.45σ horizontal. Check it against your own uncertainty model before you commit to a specification.
