Adaptive mode needs depth data: global bathymetry (section {§gbath}) or your own XYZ soundings (section {§xyz}). Each line is placed as far from the previous one as the local depth allows while keeping the chosen overlap along its whole length. The swath is assumed to see flat seabed at the depth under the line, and the lines alternate direction (port/starboard angles swap).

Depths shallower than the limit above (drying banks, very shallow water) are left out of the adaptive spacing, so a shoal at one end of a line does not pull every line close together. The plan checks say how much of the area that is.
