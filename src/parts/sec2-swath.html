Adaptive mode needs depth data: global bathymetry (section 10) or your own XYZ soundings (section 11). Each line is placed as far from the previous one as the local depth allows while keeping the chosen overlap along its whole length. The swath is assumed to see flat seabed at the depth under the line, and the lines alternate direction (port/starboard angles swap).
